Check Task is an **Android** app for tracking your tasks and goals. You write tasks into categories ("Files"), and the app reminds you with notifications so nothing slips — with your data synced to the cloud so it follows you across devices.

> The app is open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/Ahgaff-level4/Check-Task), with an [APK release](https://github.com/Ahgaff-level4/Check-Task/releases) you can install.
{: .prompt-tip}

## Features

- **Categorized tasks** — organize tasks and goals into categories ("Files").
- **Reminders** — notifications keep you on track with scheduled tasks.
- **Cloud sync** — tasks sync across devices via Firebase.
- **Google sign-in** — authentication through Firebase.

## Tech stack

A native **Android** app written in **Java** (Android Studio), backed by **Firebase** — Authentication for Google sign-in and Cloud Firestore for cloud-synced storage.

Built as a Mobile Application Development course project at Al-Ahgaff University, together with **Abdullah Al-jofri**.
